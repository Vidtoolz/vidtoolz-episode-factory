# Raw/presentation integrity oracle

Raw authentic capture and 9:16 editorial presentation are distinct files with distinct exact destinations and hashes. The presentation provenance references the raw through the evidence chain; modifying a presentation never mutates raw. Missing, aliased, corrupt, zero-byte, truncated, wrong-format, wrong-dimension, or detached files fail.

Allowed derivative operations are background framing, bounded crop/zoom/pan, traceable highlight/callout, and authorized privacy blur/redaction. They must retain protected context. Retyping, redrawing, generative fill, content replacement, removing contradictory context, or covering evidence changes meaning and is forbidden.
