User guide
What this is: one place to get soundtrack music for your videos. It plans the music from your script, renders a few versions, and hands the winner to your edit. The heavy lifting (cue structure, energy, timing, instrumentation) is automatic; you listen and decide.
Workflow: 1) Create or open a project (paste the narration script). 2) Create Music Plan — read the plain-language strategy. 3) Generate Options — listen to 2–5 versions. 4) If nothing fits, Revise with ordinary words (calmer / darker / stronger ending / less busy). 5) Use This on the winner — the approved mix, stems and MIDI are written to the project folder for Resolve.
Where files land: the approved soundtrack (mix, dialogue-safe mix, per-lane stems, MIDI, provenance) is exported into the project's music folder — for package projects that is on VIDNAS with the rest of the package.
Honest limits: the built-in preview renderer is a structural sketch (timing/energy/arrangement). MiniMax Music 3 produces production-sound candidates, but its worker starts manually and the system reports truthfully when it is offline. Generated audio remains unreviewed until you explicitly mark USE or Reject, and nothing auto-approves.
Need more control? Open the Score workspace for cue-level editing, REAPER/Ableton handoffs and production import.
