## Refreeze law
A row or schema moves `NOT_TESTED`/`DOCUMENTED_NOT_QUALIFIED` → `QUALIFIED_*`, or PROVISIONAL → FROZEN, only when all hold: (a) a `probe-<n>.json` with S0/S1 guard digests and journal exists for that exact primitive and argument shape; (b) expected and actual match, or the schema is amended to actual behaviour and re-versioned; (c) failure behaviour (False/nil/timeout/partial) is characterized; (d) concurrency implications are recorded; (e) a readback/verification path detects its effect; (f) crash recovery inside it is fault-tested. Read getters are qualified by the **M0** probe (`P0-read`) into `CAPABILITIES.json` evidence records with `version_match: true` on 21.1.0 build 14. The refreeze is a new bundle version with its own manifest and approval.
