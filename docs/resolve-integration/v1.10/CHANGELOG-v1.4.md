- **Rationale:** the independent review of v1.3 found six MAJOR defects and one MINOR (enumerated in Mikko's v1.4 mission, 25 items): (1) attachment evidence could be mixed across incompatible authority/target records; (2) attachment freshness was insertion-order dependent and stale observations could win; (3) degraded timeline observations could not be represented when required timeline fields were unavailable; (4) capability qualification evidence could be forged or accepted despite failed/stale review evidence; (5) journal APPLIED operation ids need not belong to the bound plan; (6) verification could claim success without proving the operation effect from S0→S1; (MINOR) project identity observation status/reason was not part of guard authority. Only those defects and the corrections adjudicated for them are changed.
- **Scope discipline:** no Resolve launched or mutated; no M0; no `EKA` access; no adapter; v1.0–v1.3 bytes untouched; unrelated Earth Studio work untouched.
