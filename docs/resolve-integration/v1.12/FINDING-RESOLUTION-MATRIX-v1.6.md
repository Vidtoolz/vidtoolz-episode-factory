## Gate consequences (unchanged by this bundle)

`MERGE` of v1.5 as infrastructure remains Codex's disposition; `PROBE_M0A` remains permissible in principle and **not authorized**; `REVIEW` remains raw-first with no promotion; `REFREEZE`, `ORDINARY_M0` and `PRE_M3` remain **BLOCKED** until this bundle is independently reviewed. Nothing in v1.6 is human approval of any run, and no finding here is closed by assertion: each row above names an executable negative test, or is marked as a documentation-only resolution (R10) or a contract-only resolution (R12).
